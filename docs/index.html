Know Your Opponent

The game we are playing

  • There are three piles of stones.
  • On your turn, choose one pile.
  • Remove at least one stone from that pile.
  • You may remove as many stones as you want from that one pile.
  • The player who takes the last stone wins.
The opening app hides the hidden strategy. The learning app reveals how the model searches for a balanced position.
